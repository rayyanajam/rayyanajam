Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains how Coventry Mail Hub ("we", "us") collects and uses personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or use our virtual address and mail forwarding services. We are the data controller for this information.

Contact details

Coventry Mail Hub, 1 New Union Street, Coventry CV1 2NT, United Kingdom.
Phone and WhatsApp: +44 7396 841030

What we collect

  • Inquiry form: your name, company name, email address, phone number, business type, the plan and services you're interested in, your preferred forwarding frequency, and your message.
  • Phone and WhatsApp: your phone number, name and anything you tell us when you call or message us.
  • Customers: identity and address documents for you and your company's directors, your company details, forwarding addresses, billing details, and records of the mail and parcels we receive and handle for you.
  • Website use: if you accept cookies, Google Ads records information such as which ad you clicked and whether you sent us an inquiry.

How we use it and why

  • To reply to your inquiry and send you a quote, because you asked us to (legitimate interests and steps before entering a contract).
  • To provide our services, including receiving, scanning and forwarding your mail (performance of a contract).
  • To verify your identity and keep records, as required by the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds Regulations 2017 (legal obligation).
  • To measure how well our Google ads work, only if you accept cookies (consent).

We don't sell your personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

  • Google: inquiry form submissions are stored in Google Forms, and Google Ads measures ad performance if you accept cookies.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): if you choose to message us on WhatsApp.
  • Netlify: hosts this website.
  • Royal Mail and couriers: your forwarding address, so we can send on your mail.
  • Authorities: such as HMRC or the police, when the law requires it.

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK. Where they do, they use legally approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or adequacy regulations.

How long we keep it

  • Inquiries that don't lead to a service: up to 12 months.
  • Customer records and identity checks: for as long as you're a customer, then 5 years, as anti-money laundering law requires.
  • Cookie choice: stored in your browser until you change it or clear your browser data.

Cookies

We only set advertising cookies if you choose Accept in the cookie banner. These are Google Ads cookies that tell us whether people who clicked our ads went on to contact us. If you choose Reject, they aren't set; Google may still receive basic, cookieless information that can't be used to identify you.

We store your choice in your browser so we don't ask again. You can change it at any time with .

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, to have it transferred to you or another organisation, and to withdraw consent at any time. Some rights are limited where the law requires us to keep records. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
