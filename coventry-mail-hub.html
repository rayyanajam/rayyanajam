FAQ

Common questions.

Can I use your address as my registered office with Companies House?

Yes. Our address is an appropriate address under the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act, so you can use it as your registered office and director's service address.

Why do you need to verify my ID?

Mail forwarding and registered office providers are regulated under UK anti-money laundering regulations. We must confirm the identity and home address of account holders and company directors before activating a service.

How quickly will I know when mail arrives?

Mail is logged the day it arrives and you'll receive an email notification. On scanning plans, you'll receive a PDF of the letter the same working day.

Can you forward mail abroad?

Absolutely. We forward to any UK or international address. Postage is charged at cost plus a small handling fee.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. Plans are billed monthly or annually and you can cancel at any time with 30 days' notice. Annual plans include a discount.

Can I collect my mail in person?

Yes. If you're local to Coventry, you can collect mail from us during office hours. Just let us know in advance.
